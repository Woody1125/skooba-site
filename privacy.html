Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the Skooba app for iPhone and iPad, and the Skooba app for Mac.

Skooba has no accounts and no Skooba server. Your dive log stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data, and there is no advertising or analytics in the apps.

What Skooba stores

Skooba is a dive logbook. The information you put in it — dives and depth profiles, dive sites and their GPS coordinates, gear, certificates, buddies, marine life sightings, notes and photos — is stored in a database on your device. We never receive a copy.

iCloud sync

If you turn on Cloud Sync, Skooba keeps your iPhone and Mac in step by writing sync files to your own iCloud Drive. That data is stored and handled by Apple under your Apple ID, and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. It is not sent to us.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth: to connect to your dive computer and download its dive logs. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Location: used only by the optional Location Tracker. It starts only when you tap “Start recording location” and stops when you tap “Stop recording”. While it runs, the app stores a position about every 10 minutes, so a first-time dive site can be tagged with GPS coordinates. These positions stay on your device.
  • Photos: the app reads your photo library so the Trip Album can show the photos and videos you took on the dates of a dive trip, and so you can attach photos you choose to dives, certificates, marine life and gear. Photos are displayed on your device and are not uploaded. The app does not read the location stored inside your photos. If you save an image from Skooba, it is added to your library.
  • Notifications: local reminders on your device when a piece of gear is due for servicing.

You can change or revoke any of these in the iOS Settings app. Skooba keeps working without location, photo or notification access, with the related feature turned off.

Network connections

  • iPhone and iPad: the app does not connect to any Skooba server and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Mac: the app checks for new versions by contacting a Skooba update server. This request is used only to find out whether an update exists and does not include your dive log.
  • Garmin Connect (Mac, optional): if you choose to sign in to Garmin Connect to download dives, your sign-in details are sent directly to Garmin. We never receive them. Garmin's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Sharing: if you share a dive image to Instagram or elsewhere, that is your own action and the image goes to the app you choose.

Your control over your data

Your data is yours. You can export it, back it up, or delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device; data you synced to your iCloud Drive can be removed from iCloud in the Settings app on your device.

Children

Skooba is a general-purpose logbook and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal data, we hold no information about any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
