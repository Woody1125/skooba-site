Support

Questions, a bug, or a dive computer that won't import? Email us at support@skooba.app.

Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), the Skooba version, and the diagnostic log (see how below for your device). If it's about a download, add your dive computer model.

iPhone & iPad

How do I get dives into Skooba?

Open More → Import. You can add a dive computer and download over Bluetooth, or choose a UDDF, Garmin/Suunto FIT or Suunto JSON log file under “From Log File”.

How do I sync with my Mac?

Open More → Cloud Sync and turn it on. Sync uses your own iCloud Drive, so both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple ID.

What is the Location Tracker?

It is optional. Open More → Location Tracker and tap “Start recording location” when you start a dive trip, then “Stop recording” when you finish, so Skooba can tag first-time dive sites with GPS coordinates.

Why does the Trip Album show no photos?

The Trip Album shows the photos and videos taken on the dates of the trip, so Skooba needs access to your photo library. In the iOS Settings app, open Skooba → Photos and choose All Photos. If it is set to “Limited Access”, Skooba can only see the few photos you picked, so most trip photos will not appear.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

Open More → Settings → Data → Create Backup to save a backup file. On the new phone, use Restore from backup in the same place. If you use Cloud Sync, you can also turn it on with the same Apple ID on the new phone to bring your dives over.

How do I send a diagnostic log?

Open More → Settings → Diagnostics → Export Diagnostic Log, then email us the file.

Mac

How do I get dives into Skooba?

Click Import in the toolbar. Under “From Dive Computer”, choose Bluetooth… to download from your dive computer. Under “From Log File”, choose a UDDF, MacDive XML, Skooba XML or Garmin/Suunto FIT file, or a whole folder of FIT files. Under “From Cloud”, choose Garmin Connect… to download from your Garmin account.

How do I sync with my iPhone?

Open Settings → Cloud Sync and turn it on. Sync uses your own iCloud Drive, so both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple ID.

Why does Garmin Connect say it is not accepting my sign-in?

Garmin sometimes limits sign-in attempts. Check your Garmin account details and try again in a while.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings → Backup & Restore, then choose Create Backup… to save a backup file, or Restore from Backup… to restore from one.

How do I send a diagnostic log?

Open Settings → Advanced → Diagnostics → Reveal Logs, then attach the files from the folder that opens.

General

Why is my first sync slow, or stuck waiting for iCloud?

iCloud may not have downloaded all your Skooba files to this device yet. Before you sync, set the Skooba folder in iCloud Drive to Keep Downloaded, then sync again.

  • iPhone / iPad: open the Files app → iCloud Drive, touch and hold Skooba (Do Not Modify), and tap Keep Downloaded.
  • Mac: open Finder → iCloud Drive, right-click the Skooba (Do Not Modify) folder, and choose Keep Downloaded.

Why does Sync say “up to date” when my new dives are not there?

iCloud can take a moment to show new files. Wait a few seconds and tap Sync now again.

What does “iCloud is full” mean?

Sync files are stored in your iCloud Drive, so new data cannot sync while your iCloud storage is full. Free up space or add storage in your Apple Account’s iCloud settings, then sync again.

Why is the time of my imported dives wrong?

Skooba keeps the date and time exactly as recorded in the file. Some apps and dive computers record times in their own time zone, so dives from another time zone can look shifted. You can correct a dive’s date and time by editing the dive.

Where is my data stored?

On your own devices and in your own iCloud Drive. Skooba has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
