Common questions

How do I get dives into Skooba?

On iPhone, open More → Import. You can add a dive computer and download over Bluetooth, or choose a UDDF, Garmin/Suunto FIT or Suunto JSON log file under “From Log File”.

How do I sync my iPhone and Mac?

Open More → Cloud Sync on each device and turn it on. Sync uses your own iCloud Drive, so both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple ID.

What is the Location Tracker?

It is optional. Tap “Start recording location” when you start a dive trip and “Stop recording” when you finish, so Skooba can tag first-time dive sites with GPS coordinates.

Where is my data stored?

On your own devices and in your own iCloud Drive. Skooba has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
