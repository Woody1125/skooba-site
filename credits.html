Dive site data

Skooba includes a built-in list of dive sites (names and approximate GPS coordinates) that helps match a dive to a known site. It is derived in part from data that is © OpenStreetMap contributors, and is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Dive site data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0

Part of the list was compiled with the help of dive-vibe-community, a community-maintained collection of dive site data. Other entries were added and edited by the Skooba developer.

Please note

Dive site names and coordinates are approximate and may be out of date. They are provided for logging and reference only. They are not a substitute for local knowledge, a qualified guide or official charts, and should never be your only source when planning a dive.
